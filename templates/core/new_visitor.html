Your First Sunday

What to Expect

1

Park & Arrive

Arrive 10–15 minutes early. Our friendly ushers will welcome you at the entrance and guide you to a comfortable seat.

2

Worship

Experience anointed praise and worship that ushers in the presence of God. Expect uplifting songs, both contemporary and classical.

3

The Word

Our pastor preaches practical, faith-building messages straight from the Bible that will inspire and challenge you in everyday life.

4

Connect

After service, introduce yourself to our Connect Team. We'll help you find a ministry, small group, or community to belong to.

Children's Ministry
We have safe, engaging children's church for ages 0–12. Your kids will love it while you focus on the Word.
Service Duration
Services typically run 90–120 minutes. Come expecting a full encounter with God from worship through the sermon.
Dress Code
Come as you are! While we encourage modest, neat attire, there is no strict dress code. You are welcome just as you are.
Parking
Ample parking is available on-site and at designated overflow areas nearby. Parking attendants will be on hand to assist you.

Do I need to register before coming?
Not at all! Walk-ins are always welcome. Just show up and our ushers will take care of you. You can register online to get updates and connect with the community.

What should I bring?
Just bring yourself and an open heart! A Bible is welcome but not required — scriptures are usually displayed on screen. You may also bring a notebook if you like to take notes during the message.

Is there a children's programme?
Yes! We have an excellent Children's Church for ages 0–12. There are trained, background-checked volunteers who run engaging, age-appropriate programmes while you enjoy the adult service.

Will I be put on the spot as a visitor?
Absolutely not. You are welcome to come, worship, and leave without any pressure. If you'd like to be acknowledged or connect with someone, our Connect Team will be ready — but only if you want that.
